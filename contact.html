Frequently Asked Questions

What should I expect during my first visit?

During your initial consultation, our practitioner will conduct a comprehensive evaluation of your health history, discuss your current concerns, and may perform a physical examination. We will then develop a personalized treatment plan tailored to your needs.

How long do acupuncture sessions usually take?

A typical acupuncture session lasts between 45 to 60 minutes. Your first appointment, which includes the initial consultation, may take up to 90 minutes to ensure a thorough assessment.

Do you accept insurance?

Yes, we accept many major insurance plans. Please contact our front desk with your insurance details before your appointment so we can verify your coverage for acupuncture and related therapies.
